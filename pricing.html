02 / Licensing

Hosted access is not source ownership.

Self-hosted, source-access, and exclusive-rights requests are separate transactions. They are reviewed independently from the monthly hosted plans and depend on what the current productized Scout package can support.

Case-by-case availability

Portable runtime license

From $15,000

Negotiated

03 / Process

How an early deployment is scoped.

The goal is to know the problem, expected behavior, and commercial boundaries before implementation starts.

01

Use case

Identify the business question, workflow, or customer interaction Scout is expected to handle.

02

Inputs and systems

Identify the knowledge, data, APIs, tools, authentication, and deployment constraints involved.

03

Acceptance criteria

Define what has to work, what is out of scope, and how the result will be evaluated.

04

Pilot / implementation

Configure and test the agreed scope without silently expanding the engagement into unrelated work.

05

Production decision

Move to a hosted or separately licensed model only after the deployment scope and ongoing responsibilities are understood.

04 / Pricing notes

Common commercial questions.

Specific terms are stated in the written quote, order form, or license agreement for the deployment.

Is there a public free plan?

No public free commercial plan is listed here. The current ProjectHub Scout deployment can be viewed separately as a working implementation.

Is monthly usage unlimited?

No blanket unlimited promise is made. Included usage and any additional charges are defined in the written scope before launch.

Does a hosted subscription include Scout source code?

No. Hosted access and source licensing are separate commercial models.

Does the customer own Scout after paying for setup?

Standard setup and hosted pricing do not transfer the underlying Scout source/IP. Any ownership or exclusive rights require a separate written agreement.

Can Scout be used for any industry immediately?

Use-case fit is reviewed individually. Scout is being productized for broader reuse, but current commercial availability depends on the requested domain, data, tools, and deployment requirements.

Are setup fees always exactly the amounts shown?

No. They are reference prices for the listed scope. Data cleanup, integrations, security requirements, deployment work, or custom workflows can change implementation cost.
